Part 2 0:35 – 1:15

Data Wrangling, Feature Fusion & Leakage-Safe SMOTE

Leakage-Safe Preprocessing Pipeline (CO2 / L3)

🧹 Data Audit & Cleaning

  • 26,248 raw multilingual disaster messages audited.
  • Deduplicated identical text entries to prevent cross-partition leakage.
  • Handled missing fields and normalized text casing.

⚙️ Heterogeneous Feature Fusion

  • Text Features: Unigram & Bigram TF-IDF sparse matrix (max_features=1000).
  • Urgency Metadata: Exclamation marks (!), question markers (?), uppercase intensity ratio.
  • Context Encodings: Channel genres (Direct SMS vs. News vs. Social).

🛡️ Anti-Leakage SMOTE

  • Stratified 80/20 Split: Executed before any transformer fitting.
  • Zero Data Leakage: Scalers & vectorizers fitted strictly on X_train.
  • SMOTE Oversampling: Applied solely to training split to balance minority emergency requests.

⚖️ Responsible AI & Ethical Framework

  • Asymmetric Cost Matrix: False Negatives (unattended victims) are far costlier than False Positives. We optimize for high recall.
  • Human-in-the-Loop: AI acts as an accelerator for dispatchers, never the sole decision-maker.
  • Privacy & Bias: Zero PII stored; regional dialect adaptation required for deployment.
